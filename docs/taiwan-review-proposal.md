## 1. Pre-submission inquiry(英文,可直接寄編輯)

Subject: Pre-submission enquiry — review article on Taiwan's role in the China-free drone supply chain

Dear Editors,

I am writing to ask whether the following review article would fall within the scope of Defense & Security Analysis before I prepare a full manuscript.

Working title: *The Invisible Supplier: Taiwan's Drone Exports, the Ukraine War, and the China-Free Supply Chain*

The article reviews the rapid rise of Taiwan's drone exports (an almost twentyfold year-on-year increase in early 2026 per customs data), traces their flow toward Ukraine through Polish, Czech, and U.S. intermediaries, and assesses Taiwan's first entry onto the U.S. DoD Blue UAS list. It synthesises trade statistics, think-tank reports, and industry reporting to evaluate a central tension: Taiwan can produce certified, combat-bound drones, yet its scale, upstream dependence on China, and recent defence-budget cuts constrain its wartime capacity. The contribution is an integrated assessment of a democratic non-belligerent's position in a de-risked defence supply chain, with implications for allied industrial-base policy.

The manuscript would be approximately 6,000–8,000 words. I would be grateful to know whether this fits the journal's scope and article types.

Thank you for your time.

## 2. 文章骨架(英文 review,照填即可)

- **Title**: The Invisible Supplier: Taiwan's Drone Exports, the Ukraine War, and the China-Free Supply Chain
- **核心問題**:Taiwan 在「去中化」國防無人機供應鏈中,實際扮演什麼角色?能力與限制各在哪?
- **分析框架**:能力(production / certification)對限制(scale / upstream dependence / procurement)的張力;放進「allied defence industrial base resilience」的脈絡。
- **章節**:
  1. Introduction — 用 Vyriy 採購主管那句「台灣零件已不稀奇」切入,點出「前線日常 vs 後方不屑」的張力與本文問題。
  2. Method & sources — 說明資料來源(財政部海關、DSET、業界報導)與限制(轉口流向無法海關蓋章、灰色文獻為主)。
  3. The export surge — 海關數據、DSET 數據、金額與排名(波蘭/捷克)。
  4. Where the drones go — 經第三方轉烏克蘭的研判與不確定性。
  5. Crossing the Pentagon threshold — 雷虎 Overkill 進 Blue UAS 的意義與門檻。
  6. The gaps — 規模差一個量級、上游依賴中國、無自家實戰回饋。
  7. The procurement reversal — 2026 國防特別預算砍 38%、本土無人機被刪的影響。
  8. Discussion / implications — 對盟邦供應鏈韌性政策的意涵。
  9. Conclusion — 模式歸納收尾(不喊口號)。
- **要補強的學術對話**:defence industrial base、supply chain de-risking/friend-shoring、small-state security、drone warfare 的既有文獻(回本機檢索補)。
- **關鍵來源**:沿用部落格文章的 frontmatter(DSET 報告、AFP/HKFP、IEEE Spectrum、The Diplomat、Jamestown、Defence Matters),全部轉成正式引註。
